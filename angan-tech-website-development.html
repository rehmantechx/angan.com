Website Development

Angan Tech, Anngan Technologies Pvt. Ltd, Paragon City, Lahore.

A coded business or property website, built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, designed mobile first, that loads fast on a cheap Android phone and brings enquiries to your phone.

What is included
1Pages, not a landing page. Home, About, Projects, Properties, Agencies, Blogs, Societies Guide and Contact, each a real page, with your real content, not dummy text.
2Search that works. A search bar with city, type, beds and budget filters that actually filters your real listings and projects, and never returns a blank page.
3Real photos first. Your real project photos and listing photos, resized for fast loading. No AI images, no stock renders labelled as your project.
4Your logo and colours. Your original logo on white, and your brand colours, red #DD3333 and dark blue #004274, used consistently on every page.
5Call and WhatsApp on every page. A Call button and a WhatsApp button in the header, in every listing card and on every project page, so a buyer never has to hunt for your number.
6Google ranking built in. Title, description, geo tags and RealEstateAgent schema in the page head, clean page names, and fast loading, so Google can read and rank the site.
What you get
  • A live website on your own domain or on GitHub Pages, with every page opening and every link working.
  • A list of every page, with its link, checked by hand on phone and desktop.
  • Your logo, photos and contact details on every page, in your colours.
  • A search that filters your real listings, tested with your real cities and sizes.
  • A handover note: how to add a listing, how to change a price, and who to call when something breaks.
How it works
  1. Step 1, Call: You call +92 304 1111 698 or WhatsApp us. We write down your pages, your projects, your listings and your photos.
  2. Step 2, Design first: We show you the design on a phone and on a desktop. You approve it before any code is written.
  3. Step 3, Build: We build the pages in clean code, with your real photos and your real prices, no dummy content.
  4. Step 4, Check: We tap every button, every link and every search on a real phone, and fix what is cut or broken.
  5. Step 5, Live: We push the site live and check the live link ourselves before we hand it to you.
Who it is for

Property dealers, housing projects and small businesses in Pakistan who need a real website, not a landing page, and who want buyers to call them from the site.

Questions
How long does it take?
It depends on how many pages and photos you have. We give you a date on the first call, after we have seen your content.
Do I need WordPress?
No. The site is built in clean code, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no WordPress and no monthly plugin bill.
Can I add a listing myself later?
Yes. The handover note shows you how to add a listing, and we can add it for you if you send the photo and price on WhatsApp.
