Corporate Software

Angan Tech, Anngan Technologies Pvt. Ltd, Paragon City, Lahore.

Software built around how your office actually works, for a property office, a housing project or a small company, so your team stops keeping records in notebooks and WhatsApp chats.

What is included
1One system for your office. Your staff, your files, your bookings and your payments in one place, so a file is never lost because one person is on leave.
2Built from your own forms. We start from the forms and registers your office already uses, paper or Excel, and build the software around them, not the other way round.
3Works on phone and computer. Your team opens it on a phone at a site visit and on a computer in the office, with the same records.
4Who did what, and when. Every file, every payment and every change carries the name of the person who made it and the date, so nothing is changed quietly.
5Reports your boss can read. Daily and monthly reports in plain tables: files sold, files booked, payments received, payments due.
6Your data stays yours. Your records are stored in your own account. We never sell them, share them or use them for another client.
What you get
  • A working system, tested with your own real forms and your own real records.
  • A login for each member of your team, with only the records they need to see.
  • Reports in plain tables, daily and monthly.
  • A training session for your team, in the office, in Urdu.
How it works
  1. Step 1, Call: You tell us how your office works today, and show us your forms and registers.
  2. Step 2, Plan: We write down exactly what the software will do, screen by screen, and you approve it.
  3. Step 3, Build: We build it with your real forms, and test it with your real records.
  4. Step 4, Train: We train your team in the office, and stay for the first week while they use it.
  5. Step 5, Support: When something breaks, you call us. We fix it and tell you what went wrong.
Who it is for

Property offices, housing projects and small companies whose records are still in notebooks, Excel sheets and WhatsApp chats, and who want one system their whole team uses.

Questions
Will my team be able to use it?
Yes. We build it from your own forms, so it looks like the work they already do, and we train them in the office.
What happens to my old records?
We move them in with you, file by file, and check the totals against your registers before we go live.
Is my data safe?
Your records stay in your own account. Only your team can open them.
