Content Writing

Angan Tech, Anngan Technologies Pvt. Ltd, Paragon City, Lahore.

Clear pages, project write-ups and articles written in plain words your buyers understand, in Urdu and English, with your real facts, not copied text.

What is included
1Project pages that answer the buyer. Each project page answers the same five questions: where is it, who is the developer, what sizes are there, what is the price, and who do I call. No long paragraphs that say nothing.
2Listing descriptions that sell the house. A listing description names the size, the beds, the baths, the society and the nearest landmark, in two short paragraphs, so a buyer can decide in one minute.
3Blog and news articles. Articles written from your real news, for example a soft launch, a new precinct or a payment plan, with the real date and a link to the project page.
4Urdu and English. Every page written in plain English, with Urdu where your buyers read Urdu, and the same facts in both, never two different stories.
5No copied text. Every word written for your project, from your facts. No copied text from Zameen, Graana or another dealer, and no AI filler.
6Checked before it goes live. You read every page before it is published. Nothing goes live with a wrong price, a wrong size or a wrong phone number.
What you get
  • Finished pages, ready to publish, in plain Urdu and English.
  • A list of the facts each page is based on, so you can check them.
  • Corrections made before publishing, not after a buyer has read the mistake.
How it works
  1. Step 1, Call: You tell us the project and send the facts: sizes, prices, location, developer.
  2. Step 2, Draft: We write the page and send it to you on WhatsApp to read.
  3. Step 3, Correct: You correct any fact that is wrong. We fix it the same day.
  4. Step 4, Live: The page goes live only after you approve the words.
Who it is for

Property dealers and housing projects who need pages buyers can actually read, in plain Urdu and English, with the right price and the right phone number.

Questions
Who writes the Urdu?
We do, in plain Urdu, from the same facts as the English page, so both pages agree.
Can you write about my project if I only have a brochure?
Yes. Send the brochure on WhatsApp. We write from it, and we ask you about anything the brochure does not say.
What if a price changes?
Tell us on WhatsApp. We change the price on every page that shows it, the same day.
