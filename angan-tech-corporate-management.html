Corporate Management

Angan Tech, Anngan Technologies Pvt. Ltd, Paragon City, Lahore.

One dashboard for tasks, approvals and reporting across departments, so the owner sees what every department did today, and nothing waits for a signature that nobody knows about.

What is included
1One task list for the whole company. Every task has a name, a department, a person, a due date and a status. The dashboard shows what is done, what is late and what has no owner.
2Approvals in the open. A payment, a discount or a file transfer goes for approval in the system. The approver sees it, approves or refuses it with a reason, and the decision is recorded.
3Reports by department. Each department reports the same way: what was done today, what is stuck, and what it needs. The owner reads all of them on one page.
4Documents in one place. Letters, approvals, payment slips and agreements are attached to the task or the file they belong to, so they are found in one search.
5Meetings with decisions. A meeting ends with decisions written down, each with a person and a date, and the dashboard shows which decisions are still open.
6One dashboard for the owner. One page, every morning: tasks done, tasks late, approvals waiting, and reports from every department.
What you get
  • A dashboard showing tasks, approvals and reports from every department, on one page.
  • A task list with owner, due date and status, for every department.
  • An approval record: who asked, who approved, and when.
  • A training session for your team, in the office, in Urdu.
How it works
  1. Step 1, Call: You tell us your departments, and how work and approvals move between them today.
  2. Step 2, Plan: We write down the task list, the approval steps and the daily report, and you approve it.
  3. Step 3, Build: We build the dashboard around your departments, with your real tasks.
  4. Step 4, Live: Your departments report in the system from the next week, with us on the phone for the first week.
Who it is for

Companies with more than one department, where the owner cannot see what each department did today, and approvals wait because nobody knows they are waiting.

Questions
Will my departments have to change how they work?
No. We build the task list and the approvals around the way you already work, department by department.
Who can approve a payment?
Only the people you name. The system records who approved it and when, so the decision is never in doubt.
What if a task has no owner?
The dashboard shows it as having no owner, in red, so the owner can assign it the same day.
