Calculators and guides that work in your browser. No account, no payment.

Construction Cost Calculator

Enter your covered area and the rate per sqft your contractor quoted. The calculator multiplies them, and adds a 10 percent contingency if you tick the box. It does not invent a rate: ask your contractor for today's rate and type it in.

Home Loan Calculator

Monthly payment on a reducing-balance loan. Enter the amount, the number of years and the annual markup rate your bank quoted.
