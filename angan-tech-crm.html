CRM

Angan Tech, Anngan Technologies Pvt. Ltd, Paragon City, Lahore.

A CRM that tracks every lead from the first call to the closing, so no enquiry is lost, and every dealer knows exactly which buyer to call today.

What is included
1Every lead in one list. Every call, every WhatsApp message and every walk-in is entered as a lead, with name, phone, city, budget and what they want, the same day it comes in.
2Follow-up dates, not memory. Each lead gets a follow-up date. The CRM shows your team, every morning, the leads to call today, and the ones that are already late.
3One lead, one owner. Each lead belongs to one dealer. No two dealers call the same buyer, and no lead sits with nobody.
4From enquiry to closing. A lead moves through clear stages: new, called, visited, booked, closed, or lost, with the reason written down when it is lost.
5WhatsApp and call from the lead. Your team calls or WhatsApps the buyer straight from the lead, and the call and the message are recorded on the lead.
6Daily report for the owner. At the end of the day, one table: new leads, calls made, follow-ups done, follow-ups missed, bookings and closings.
What you get
  • A lead list with every enquiry, its owner, its stage and its next follow-up date.
  • A daily follow-up list for each dealer, showing who to call today.
  • A daily report for the owner: new leads, calls, follow-ups, bookings and closings.
  • A training session for your team, in the office, in Urdu.
How it works
  1. Step 1, Call: You tell us how leads come to you today, by call, WhatsApp or walk-in, and how many dealers you have.
  2. Step 2, Setup: We set up the lead stages, the follow-up rules and the dealer list with you.
  3. Step 3, Test: We enter your real leads from the last week, and your team calls them from the CRM.
  4. Step 4, Live: From the next day, every new lead goes into the CRM the same day it comes in.
Who it is for

Property dealers and housing projects who get enquiries on WhatsApp and by phone, and lose buyers because nobody follows up on time.

Questions
What if a dealer leaves?
Their leads stay in the CRM. You give them to another dealer, with the full history, so no buyer is lost.
Do I need to change how I work?
No. The CRM follows your stages: new, called, visited, booked, closed. We set it up the way you already sell.
Can I see what my team did today?
Yes. The daily report shows every call, every follow-up and every booking, by dealer.
